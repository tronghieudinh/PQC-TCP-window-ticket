# Round 3d: a registered confirmation of round 3c (23 Nov 2026; criteria APPROVED by the authors on 23 Nov 2026 (the GX10 run started at 05:06 after the approval); thresholds derived from the data of round 3c and committed in 8f8e3c9 before any 3d measurement)

Written for: the authors. Follows `r3c_findings.md`: round 3c (the write of the response delayed) moved the order on both machines as predicted, and the late share at each delay followed from the transactions measured without the intervention (mean error 2.1 points on the X250 against 33.1 for no dependence on D), but two registered criteria were missed on the X250 (C1: the timer of `nanosleep` added 88 to 229 us against the 154 us derived on the Arm pilot; C3: one cell of 24). Round 3d is the confirmation, on NEW data of both machines, with the two causes removed. Time box: one day; the paper changes only if the criteria are met (the clause "explored" becomes "tested").

## What changes (and nothing else)
1. The instrument: `tls_probe server --pre-write-spin-us D` waits by SPINNING on `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` until the deadline (no timer slack): the write should be later by D within a few microseconds on any machine (checked on the loopback: +50 ms for D = 50 000 us). Same race design (request 25 000 B, RTT 100 ms, 100 Mbit/s, MTU 1 500 and 1 280, ECDSA-P256, ML-DSA-65 and -87, n = 30, `ticket_ack2.bt`, CPU clock fixed).
2. The prediction: the late share at D is predicted from the D = 0 transactions of the same series and the DISTRIBUTION of the measured shifts of the write at D (the p_w of each transaction at D minus the median p_w at D = 0), instead of the median shift alone. No fitted parameter. This definition was made after seeing the data of 3c (it brings the X250's 24 of 24 cells into the band, mean error 0.7 points, and the Arm pilot's 28 of 28 at 2.6), so 3c is the training data and 3d the test.
3. The delays, dense where the late share falls: X250 off, 0, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 800, 1 600 us; GX10 off, 0, 50, 100, 150, 200, 300, 400 us.

## Criteria (thresholds DERIVED from the data of 3c by the rules named; both machines are measured anew)
- C1 (instrument): the median of p_w at each D within 25 us of (median at D = 0 + D); the spread at most the spread at D = 0 + 0.29 x D. A prediction that can fail: the floor of the rule for a spin-wait, not a value taken from a machine; 0.29 is the slope of the 3c pilot.
- C2 (the order moves): as in 3c: a series with a late share of at least 10 % at D = 0 ends at the largest D with at most half of it; a series below 10 % must not rise by more than the binomial band (17.9 points for n = 30).
- C3 (the explanation): (a) the cells (series x delay) outside the binomial 95 % band 1.96 x sqrt(0.25 / n) around the predicted late share number at most the failures that a machine like the 52 training cells (all inside; 95 % lower bound of the share 0.944) stays under with probability 95 %: 4 for 28 to 36 cells (`r3d_thresholds.md`); (b) the mean absolute error is at most twice the largest training mean error (5.2 points) and at most 0.79 (twice the largest training ratio of the two) of the error of "no dependence on D".
- C4: the rule per window event and the burst accounting, with the thresholds of `r3b_thresholds.md` (99.914 % and 99.6 %).
- C5: C1 to C4 on both machines.
Readings (written beforehand): C1 to C4 met on a machine: the order is set by the times of the acknowledgments and of the write, moved by an intervention on that machine and predicted from the unperturbed transactions and the measured shifts: CONFIRMED (levels A and B). C1 not met: the instrument is not within the registered precision; nothing else is concluded. C1 and C2 met, C3 not met: the order moves but is not predicted as registered; the exploration of 3c stands as reported, the confirmation fails. C2 not met: the write does not set the order there (a finding).
