# Round 3b: the kernel's window rule read ACK by ACK, under the intervention (22-23 Nov 2026; protocol APPROVED by the authors on 23 Nov 2026; the thresholds were derived from the GX10 pilot on 23 Nov 2026 and committed in `r3b_thresholds.md` and `r3b_thresholds.json` BEFORE any 3b data of the X250 was opened; the X250 is the out-of-sample test, `r3b_test.py`)

Written for: the authors. Follows `r3_gx10_findings.md` (round 3 NOT SUPPORTED as registered, on both machines). Rule 5 of `METHOD_ITERATIVE.md`: new criteria, new data. The first version of this file proposed thresholds from judgment; four of them failed on the data of round 3 itself (B3's spread clause, B4's 50 points, B5's ceiling of 11, and B2's 90 % without a basis). This version fixes the PROTOCOL, and every threshold is either derived from the pilot by a stated rule or marked as a judgment.

## What round 3 showed that fixes the design
1. The delay moves the median of the ACKs' processing time by delta + about 30 us on the GX10 (the largest deviation of the median from delta + the baseline: 14 us over six levels); the spread of the per-transaction shift grows with delta (10th-90th percentile 206 us at 0, 840 at 800, 1 667 at 1 600) and about a tenth of the ACKs escape the full delay. The criterion must be on the median and on a spread that may grow with delta.
2. The delay touches every pure ACK, not only the ticket's. The test of the rule is therefore made on every window event of the connection (the events are measured), and the ECDSA control is read with the same accounting, not with a fixed ceiling.
3. The range of delta must reach the transition on each machine: up to 1.6 ms on the GX10, to 6.4 ms on the X250 (where ML-DSA-65 was never late and ML-DSA-87 always late up to 1.6 ms).
4. The burst is not the congestion window after the last ACK (burst - cwnd was 0 in 42 % of the ML-DSA transactions, -1 to -4 otherwise): the accounting of the burst is a thing to discover on the pilot, then to test.

4. A declared repeat on one machine for the noise floor (reported, not a criterion).

## Criteria and how each threshold comes
- B1 (the rule, ACK by ACK): the share of window events whose `cwnd` after equals the rule value (`tcp_reno_cong_avoid`, `tcp_slow_start`, `tcp_is_cwnd_limited`, with the counters the kernel held). THRESHOLD: derived: the largest share t such that a machine behaving like the pilot (rate = the 95 % lower Clopper-Pearson bound of the pilot's share) passes with probability 95 % over the number of events of the test. A failure means the instrument or the reading of the counters is wrong, not the physics.
- B2 (the burst from the measured events): the share of ML-DSA transactions whose burst equals the accounting found in the pilot. THRESHOLD: derived by the same binomial rule from the pilot's match rate of that accounting, on transactions of the test; and the accounting itself is fixed before the test (step 2), with its number of terms and their level (A to C).
- B3 (instrument): at every delta > 0 the median of AK - RX of the ticket's ACKs equals the median at delta 0 plus delta within a tolerance; the spread (10th-90th percentile) is at most the spread at delta 0 plus a slope times delta. THRESHOLDS: derived from the pilot: tolerance = twice the largest deviation of the pilot's medians (at least 25 us); slope = 1.5 times the largest pilot slope of the spread against delta.
- B4 (the order moves; the test is not vacuous): on each machine, for ML-DSA-65 and -87 at MTU 1 500, the late share at the largest delta exceeds that at delta 0 by at least a fraction of the pilot's difference. THRESHOLD: half of the smallest difference of the pilot's series (judgment: the fraction one half; the series of round 3 on the GX10 gave 47 to 78 points).
- B5 (control): ECDSA-P256 (n = 1) read with the accounting of B2 (the burst follows from the events), not with a ceiling: THRESHOLD as B2.
- B6 (machines): B1 and B2 hold on both machines (the pilot's and the test's), and the shares of B2 differ by at most 10 points (judgment, as R5 of round 3, which was met at 1.6 points).
Readings (written beforehand): B1, B2 and B4 met: the burst is the outcome of the kernel's rules applied to the order of the acknowledgments, by an intervention (KEPT, levels A and B). B1 met, B2 not met: the window follows the rule, the accounting of the burst is incomplete (IMPROVEMENT ONLY, the missing element named from the events). B1 not met: the instrument or the reading is wrong, nothing else is concluded. B4 not met on a machine: the range did not reach the transition there, nothing about the order is concluded for it.
